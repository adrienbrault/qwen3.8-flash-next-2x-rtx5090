# R790: after a forced end of a looping thought, 12 of 32 answers repeat the looped line at the sampler fallback 1.0 and 0 of 16 at 0.6; on one real agent turn that starts inside a loop, 0.6 continues the loop 8 of 8 times and 1.0 0 of 8; neither is a loop-incidence figure

Results `2026-09-28-r790-loopthink-temp-0103` on the serving host (2026-09-28 01:03 to 01:09 UTC). Driver: [`scripts/r790-loopthink-temp.sh`](../../scripts/r790-loopthink-temp.sh). Probe: `replay_hermes_turn.py` with `--temperature` (a later revision of [`bench/replay_hermes_turn.py`](../replay_hermes_turn.py)). Raw records: on the serving host only. The `p107` records hold the reasoning, content and tool calls of a private agent session and stay private; the tables below are the summary.

## Setup

- Client-only, on the served configuration of 2026-09-28 (image `tabbyapi:rebase-dev-r3`, 39 engine keys, loop-think r4, sampler preset temperature 1.0, top_k 20, top_p 0.95, `force: false`). Batches in the order explicit 1.0, explicit 0.6, no temperature sent | `p107` 1.0, 0.6 | explicit 0.6, no temperature sent, explicit 1.0.
- **forced**: 40 copies of one line prefilled in the thinking and a question ("17*23"), 12,000 maximum tokens, n 8 per batch, 4 concurrent. The collector's detector fires at 800 reasoning tokens and forces `</think>`.
- **p107**: turn 16 of a private agent session (R781), with the reasoning prefilled up to the point where a third copy of a repeated paragraph starts. This measures whether the model continues a loop it is already in, not whether it enters one. n 8 per arm.

## Forced loop

| arm | clean "391" | looped line in the answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| explicit 1.0 | 10 / 16 | 6 / 16 |
| no temperature sent (fallback 1.0) | 10 / 16 | 6 / 16 |
| explicit 0.6 | 16 / 16 | 0 / 16 |

- 1.0 pooled 12 of 32 against 0 of 16 at 0.6, Fisher p = 0.004; Wilson 95 % interval at 1.0 23 to 55 %. With R789's arm on the 42-key configuration added, 19 of 48 against 0 of 32.
- The explicit 1.0 and the fallback give the same counts, and the server log shows the same sampler for both, so R789's open confound is the temperature value.
- All 48 rows have byte-identical reasoning: 80 generated copies, then the injected message. The model left the loop by itself in 0 of 32 rows at 1.0 and 0 of 16 at 0.6. The two outcomes are "\n\n391" at 826 tokens, or 160 looped lines (7,042 characters) at 2,422 tokens, where the engine's content backstop `(2W, 4)` ends the request with `finish_reason` stop. The difference between the arms is one sampling decision right after the forced `</think>`, on a 10-token line.

## p107: continuing a loop on one agent turn

| arm | rows that kept repeating text | injected by loop-think | ended in a parseable tool call |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.6 | 8 / 8 | 7 / 8 | 8 / 8 |
| 1.0 | 0 / 8 | 0 / 8 | 8 / 8 |

- Fisher p = 0.0002 for repeating text. Repetition is the share of 12-word shingles in the reasoning that occur more than once: 0.72 to 1.00 on every 0.6 row, 0.00 to 0.22 on every 1.0 row.
- At 0.6, six rows were injected within about 1,200 tokens; one only after about 13,000 tokens of a longer-period repetition; one was never injected: it repeated planning lines for about 17,000 tokens with a period that no detector sees, then left the repetition by itself. The driver's summary counts that row as "not injected, not escaped", because its escape column only matches the one repeated paragraph.
- At 1.0 the reasoning ran about 2,400 to 8,000 tokens (median about 5,000, estimated from characters at 3.22 per token) and took 40 to 98 s per row, against about 8 s for the fast-injected 0.6 rows.
- The turn's pending edit (a wrong argument in one function call) was made in 4 of 8 rows at 1.0 and 1 of 8 at 0.6 (Fisher p = 0.28, not significant). The injected 0.6 rows carried out the plan the looping paragraph had been repeating.
- Server log: 55 reasoning-loop injections = 48 forced + 7 `p107` at 0.6; 12 content loop stops = the 12 looped forced rows; no other client's requests.

## What this does not show

- That 1.0 leaves loops by itself in general: on the forced line loop it left 0 of 32.
- That 1.0 recovers worse from loops in general: the forced arm is one decision on a toy loop.
- Any rate at which loops start, at either temperature: the forced arm forces entry and `p107` starts inside a loop, on one turn whose prefix was generated by a finetune of this model. [R791](r791-temp-incidence.md) replays ordinary turns without a prefix.
- That 1.0 is at least as good on real traffic.

The fallback stayed at 1.0.
