# R574: a 4,096-token prefill chunk does not fit beside the page pool

Results directory on the serving host: `results/2026-09-19-r574-chunk4096`. Raw records: [`2026-09-19-r574-chunk4096/`](2026-09-19-r574-chunk4096/). Driver: [`scripts/r574-chunk4096.sh`](../../scripts/r574-chunk4096.sh).

Served chunk 2048 against 4096, everything else equal, 8 slots at 966,656 with the NVMe tier off. The 4,096 arm does not boot: `RuntimeError: Insufficient VRAM in split for model and cache`. The deterministic grouped-MoE prefill's scratch at that chunk does not fit beside the pool, so a larger chunk would have to be paid for in page pool.

The served arm's baseline, three salted cold prefills each, prompt tokens per second:

| prompt | 30k | 60k | 120k |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| served, chunk 2048 | 9,409 | 9,814 | 10,027 |

It also survives the loaded case: a cold 120k-token prefill started three seconds into a 4-stream decode round leaves the server alive and the round at 200.9 t/s aggregate.
