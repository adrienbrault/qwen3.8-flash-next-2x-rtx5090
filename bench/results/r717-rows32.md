## The change

The served policy `[[4, 3], [5, 2], [8, 1]]` verified 6, 7 and 8 jobs at draft depth 1 (12, 14 and 16 rows), because deeper drafts exceed the 16 rows the cooperative MoE decode kernels take ([R560](r560-c8-policy.md), [R562](r562-profile-c8.md)). rows32 gives the decode path a 17-to-32-row route so that 6, 7 and 8 jobs verify at depth 2 (18, 21 and 24 rows). The candidate is four launcher changes on the rows32 image:

| key | value | what it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `EXL3_DENSE_ROWS32` | `1` | densegemm r2's one-pass 17-to-32-row twins of the dense gemm and mgemm kernels; already in `tabbyapi:stack-r3`, off in its launcher ([R714](r714-densegemm-r2.md)) |
| `EXL3_MOE_COOP_ROWS32` | `1` | the routed-expert cooperative MoE decode in one launch at 17 to 32 rows (`MAX_BSZN` and the coop cap 16 → 32); this patch |
| `EXL3_SHARED_EXPERT_ROWS32` | `1` | the shared expert's `BC_GatedMLP` graphs and statics sized for 32 rows; this patch |
| `EXL3_MOE_COOP_V3_MAP` | `2-4:2,17-32:2` (was `2-4:2`) | pins mode 2 at 17 to 32 rows; mode 3's r2 kernels stop at 16 rows and fall back to mode 2 there already ([`ANALYSIS.md`](../../docker/overlays/rows32-r4/ANALYSIS.md) §3) |
| `DRAFT_POLICY` | `[[4, 3], [8, 2]]` (was `[[4, 3], [5, 2], [8, 1]]`) | depth 3 up to 4 jobs, depth 2 at 5 to 8 jobs: 15, 18, 21 and 24 verify rows at 5, 6, 7 and 8 jobs |
| `CACHE` | 983,040 (was 999,424) | pays for the flags' 48 MiB on GPU 0 (R717b below) |

The patch touches 9 files and changes no device code: the rebuilt extension keeps the SASS of all 1,633 functions of the `stack-r3` build and adds none. It also bounds-checks `start_shared` (moefast r3's early shared-expert entry point) and doubles densegemm's side-branch slot region to 4 MiB when `EXL3_DENSE_ROWS32` is on. With every flag unset the image runs the `stack-r3` paths.

The unit printed `DECISION rows32 r4: REJECT` with four reasons ([`decision.txt`](2026-09-24-r717-rows32-r4/decision.txt)). An independent review of the round assigned them as follows:

1. `parity-cpuflags.log: no PARITY PASS`: the decision script looked for the parity suites' success line in the CPU-test log, whose success line is `CPU TESTS PASS`, which the audit shows. A script defect.
2. `c7d2 R32 round 1-4: no hashes`: the harness out-of-memory above; the served 7-stream boots ran depth 2 without errors. Settled by R717b.
3. `headroom card 0: B 985 < A 1033 - 32 MiB`: a real, deterministic breach of the rule in [`docs/PROMOTION.md`](../../docs/PROMOTION.md) (GPU 0 no more than 32 MiB below the served configuration), 48 MiB against 32. Settled by the pool (R717b).
4. `greedy_conc ... B early divergences 19 > worst A half 6 x 2`: the rate as the promotion condition states it (B no more divergent than the served configuration's own 8-stream against 1-stream variation) is met, 89.1 % against 92.2 %. The early sub-rule compares counts of about 6 per 32 prompts with no margin; under identical distributions it fires in about 21 to 27 % of draws (simulated), and a paired McNemar test on these prompts gives p = 0.092 at the 64-character cut and p ≥ 0.34 at 16, 32, 128 and 256 characters. The 10 prompts that diverge early only in B diverge at role-token and think-tag choices and between fluent alternatives, the same kinds of positions where A diverges.

The overlay's `rows32_decide.py` reads the CPU-test success line and `greedy_conc.py` reports the early count without judging it; re-deciding R717 with them leaves only reasons 2 and 3.

The pre-registered rule read `MARGINAL`: the mean over the 16,000 and 32,000 cells is 1.033 and no cell mean is below 1.00. Of the 36 individual pairs, four are below 1.000, from 0.994 to 0.999.

- **6 streams at the 4,000 setting.** The review mapped the server's draft counters to the cells: at depth 1 most of these streams accept every draft, 1.99 to 2.00 tokens per step, the ceiling of depth 1; at depth 2 they reach 2.82 to 2.91 (×1.43) for a step 1.23 times longer (15.55 → 19.1 ms). That gives the +16 to +21 %. The content is near-deterministic continuation, so this cell is an upper bound for predictable output, not the typical gain. The 8-stream cells at the same setting use other prompts, accept 1.77 to 1.99 tokens per step at depth 1 and gain 2 to 5 %.
- **Context.** The gain is the ratio of accepted tokens per step (depth 2 over depth 1) divided by the ratio of step times. The step ratio grows with context at 8 streams: 1.21 to 1.22 at the 4,000 setting, about 1.21 at 16,000, 1.27 (code) and 1.25 (prose) at 32,000. At 8 streams prose at 32,000 the two ratios are equal (1.25) and the cell reads 1.004; 6 streams code at 16,000 reads 1.005. Prose above the 32,000 setting was not measured.
- **Prefix cache and time to first token.** The recorded rounds are 92 to 100 % cached in both arms, and time to the first token matches between arms (6 streams, code, 4,000 setting: 0.73 to 0.75 s in both).
- **Repeatability.** The three A boots agree within 0.6 to 2.3 % per cell, except 6 streams prose at the 4,000 setting, 4.5 %, where A3 accepted 1.91 tokens per step; the P boots agree as closely.
- **Headroom.** Free VRAM at boot P 1,125 / 2,513 MiB and A 1,033 / 2,421 in every boot; after the 32,000 runs P 205 to 207 / 1,681 to 1,701 and A 145 to 149 / 1,659 to 1,663. 0 out-of-memory lines, TORCH_CHECK or tracebacks in the six boots. Eight streams of code at the 32,000 setting hold about 342,000 live tokens.

## Verdict

Promoted 2026-09-24 23:05 UTC (2026-09-25 01:05 CEST) with the user's agreement to the pool: the launcher's `DAILY_IMG` is `tabbyapi:stack-r3-rows32`, its default environment carries 42 keys (the 39 of `stack-r3` with the MAP changed, plus the three flags), `DRAFT_POLICY` is `[[4, 3], [8, 2]]` and `CACHE` 983,040. The promotion boot read free VRAM 1,125 / 2,513 MiB at the UP line, the lcguard line on both devices and the greedy set identical to R717's reference on 6 of 6 prompts. Rollback is `IMG=tabbyapi:stack-r3 CACHE=999424 DRAFT_POLICY='[[4, 3], [5, 2], [8, 1]]'` with the `stack-r3` 39-key `EXTRA_ENV` (MAP `2-4:2`, no ROWS32 keys).

The gain at 6 to 8 streams is +3 to +5 % per stream on 110-token prompts (R717), about +3 % with a range of 0 to +6 % on 12,000 to 44,000-token prompts (R717c), and up to +16 to +21 % at 6 streams on highly predictable 3,000 to 6,000-token prompts. 1 to 5 streams run the same kernels and draft depth as before and read 0.995 to 1.003.

Open, not blocking:

- A draft policy that depends on context length as well as on the number of jobs, since the depth-2 gain shrinks as the step ratio grows with context.
- The early-divergence sub-rule of `greedy_conc.py` stays unjudged; settling it needs more boot pairs with an 8-stream A/A control and a pre-registered paired test.
- Two TN256 M32 mgemm twins among densegemm r2's rows32 kernels spill registers; `FRAG_STAGES=2` for them is a follow-up with its own SASS and parity round ([`ANALYSIS.md`](../../docker/overlays/rows32-r4/ANALYSIS.md) §5).
