## Spec-Bench

One boot, 120 frozen stratified prompts and all 480 prompts at c1/c4/c8, T=0, 256 forced tokens and template-default thinking. Server counters record zero cached prompt tokens. Output is completion tokens divided by wall time. Per-stream rate is 1,000 divided by median TPOT in milliseconds. Acceptance is accepted drafts divided by proposed drafts. τ divides generated by generated minus accepted, including the prefill token. The selector and prompt hashes are in the [input manifest](2026-11-26-headline-r2/specbench-manifest.json).

The counted tokens are aligned post hoc to streamed text boundaries and match server usage at completion.
